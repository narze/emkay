# Product

## Register

product

## Users

MKONE members checking their current membership card on a phone at a restaurant. They need to present a live QR code, check tier points, and understand the privileges available across MK Restaurant Group brands.

## Product Purpose

Give members one dependable place to present their MKONE membership, confirm their points and tier, and review tier benefits before ordering or paying.

## Brand Personality

Familiar, direct, dependable. The interface should feel like an official MK Restaurant Group membership card, with restrained use of the MK red accent and clear everyday language.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic loyalty dashboard, a promotional landing page, or the previous under-construction placeholder. Avoid decorative UI that competes with the card and QR code.

## Design Principles

- Put the live membership card and QR code first.
- Make points, tier, and update time readable at a glance.
- Keep tier comparisons predictable and easy to scan.
- Use MKONE brand assets as the source of visual identity.
- Keep the mobile task usable without hiding information behind extra steps.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard access. Respect reduced-motion preferences, keep tap targets usable on phones, and never rely on color alone to show the selected tier.
